When I make music I'm in dialogue with silence, patience, and wonder.
I experiment with acoustic and electronic sounds; with lyrics of my own, stolen, or borrowed.

I don't forget my roots (which point south), but the rest diverges in other directions.

I'm a bassist and composer by training, a multi-instrumentalist by trade.

You can listen to the music I make on <a href="https://annarresti.bandcamp.com/album/annarresti" target="_blank">Bandcamp</a>, <a href="https://open.spotify.com/artist/5kxqHCd1vkhqKeP1IGQ0aU" target="_blank">Spotify</a> or <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCd9sxgik3BXwwylnlBLEt6g" target="_blank">YouTube</a>.
